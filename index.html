The Curious Inheritance

The fears we're handed, and the curiosity that frees us

by Aaqhil Hussain

The unlucky hour. The horoscope that seems to know you better than you know yourself. The lemon crushed under a new car, just in case. You've never really believed any of it, and you've never quite stopped doing it either. The Curious Inheritance is about where these inherited fears come from, and what they quietly cost us. You won't be asked to give anything up, only to ask one question, the way a curious child asks it: Why?

The author

Aaqhil Hussain is a writer in Melbourne. A network engineer by trade and endlessly curious by nature, he writes for anyone who has ever wanted to know why. He believes curiosity is the best thing we can hand to the people who come after us, and every book he writes is an attempt to pass a little of it on.
